# Privacy Policy for PackLite

**Last Updated: April 3, 2026**

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how PackLite ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect
PackLite is designed to be a local-first application. 
- **Personal Data**: We do not collect your name, email, or profile photo on our servers. All profile information is stored locally on your device.
- **Trip Data**: Your destinations, packing lists, and "Travel Vault" documents (passports, tickets) are encrypted and stored locally on your device. We do not have access to these files.
- **AI Concierge**: When you chat with our AI Concierge, your queries are processed to provide travel recommendations. These queries are processed through third-party AI providers (NVIDIA) but are not linked to your personal identity.

## 2. Amazon Associates Disclosure
PackLite is a participant in the **Amazon Associates Program**, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.in.

As part of this program:
- When you click on a "Get on Amazon" link within the app, your browser will open with an affiliate tracking tag (`pradeep21082-21`).
- Amazon may use cookies to track these clicks and attribute purchases to our account for commission purposes.
- Using these links does NOT increase the price of the products for you.

## 3. Third-Party Services
- **Weather Services**: We use third-party APIs to fetch real-time weather for your destination.
- **Currency Conversion**: We use third-party APIs for real-time exchange rates.
These services do not receive any of your personal data.

## 4. Data Security
We take security seriously. 
- All Travel Vault documents are encrypted.
- We recommend using biometric lock on your device to prevent unauthorized access to the app.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via the GitHub repository where the project is hosted.
